How to get the Grok images into Figma — detailed steps
Written for someone who hasn't done this before. If any step is unclear, paste the matching prompt (in the grey boxes) into Claude
or ChatGPT and it'll walk you through your exact screen.
Step 1. Turn a video into a still image (skip if Caleb sent PNGs already).
If Caleb sends an .mp4, you need one frozen frame from it. Easiest way: open the video, pause on the clearest
moment where you can see the villain and the labels, and take a screenshot (Mac: Cmd-Shift-4 and drag a box;
Windows: Win-Shift-S). Save it. Cleaner way if you have CapCut: drop the video on the timeline, move the
playhead to the frame you want, and use Export frame / Save frame as image. Either gives you a PNG.
Step 2. Open your Figma file and make a phone-sized frame for the screen you're designing.
In Figma, press F (the Frame tool), then in the right-hand panel pick a phone size (e.g. iPhone 14 / 390×844). Each
screen of the app — login, codex, card, etc. — gets its own frame. Name each frame after the screen so Caleb can
tell them apart.
Step 3. Put the image on the frame.
Simply drag the PNG from your desktop and drop it onto the frame. Figma places it as an image you can drag to
reposition and pull the corners to resize. Hold Shift while resizing to keep it from stretching.
Step 4. Or use the image as a background fill (nice for card screens).
Draw a rectangle over the frame (press R, drag it out). With the rectangle selected, in the right panel find Fill, click
the color swatch, change the dropdown from Solid to Image, and choose your PNG. Now the art fills the card
cleanly.
Step 5. Add the text and buttons on top.
Press T and click to add text (the organism name, the question, button labels). Draw rectangles for buttons. You're
not building anything real here — you're drawing what it should look like so Caleb can build it.
Step 6. Keep it organized for the handoff.
Keep every screen on its own named frame, and don't flatten things — leave images, text, and buttons as separate
layers so Caleb can see exactly what each piece is. That's what makes it a spec he can build from.
